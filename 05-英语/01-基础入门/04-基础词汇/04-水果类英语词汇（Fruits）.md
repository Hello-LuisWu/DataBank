# 🍓 常见水果词根规律

|词根|含义|示例|
|---|---|---|
|apple|苹果|apple, pineapple|
|berry|浆果|strawberry, blueberry|
|fruit|水果|dragon fruit, passion fruit|
|melon|瓜类|watermelon, honeydew melon|
|nut|坚果|coconut（椰子不是严格坚果）|
